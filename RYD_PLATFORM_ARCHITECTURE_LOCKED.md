# RYD Platform Architecture — LOCKED

**This document defines the canonical flow. Do not change this structure.**

---

## Hierarchy (Fixed)

```
12 Gates (anchors)
    └── 40 Pain Points per gate (searchable)
            └── 3 Tool Cards per pain point
                    └── Each tool: 5 min, 15 min, 30 min workthroughs
                            └── How It Works & Why It Works (cited research)
```

---

## 1. Gates (12 anchors) — NON-NEGOTIABLE HOMEPAGE

- **12 gates** on the homepage — FIRST, above Tool of the Day
- Entry points for navigation
- Users select a gate to view 40 pain points
- Example: Men's Mental Health, Women's Mental Health, Addiction Recovery, Fathers & Sons, Teen & Gen Z, Grief & Loss, etc.

---

## 2. Pain Points (40 per gate)

- **40 pain points** per gate
- **Searchable** — users find pain points via search
- Active via search, gate selection, or direct navigation

---

## 3. Tool Cards (3 per pain point)

- **3 tool cards** per pain point
- Each card links to one tool with its own workthroughs
- Three relevant tools per pain point

---

## 4. Workthroughs (5, 15, 30 min per tool) — 9 total per pain point

- **Each tool** has exactly three workthrough variants:
  - **5 min** — Quick Reset / Quick Push
  - **15 min** — Standard Practice
  - **30 min** — Deep Workthrough
- These are **tool-specific**, not generic boilerplate
- Content is generated per tool using title, problem, howWhyWorks, summary

**Per pain point: 3 tools × 3 workthroughs = 9 workthroughs total**

---

## 5. How It Works & Why It Works (cited research)

- **How It Works** — methodology of the tool
- **Why It Works** — cited research backing the methodology
- This section **never changes** in structure — it is the research citation layer for each tool

## Vocabulary (LOCKED — Do Not Use)

**Never use:** evidence-based, research-backed, clinical, helps you, will help you, proven techniques

**Use instead:** lived experience, self-help, practical tools, rooted in lived experience

**No collection of personal data.** No signup, no email capture, no user accounts.
